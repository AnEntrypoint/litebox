Every layer except the last is ~1% aligned and therefore pays the full
26.6 ms/exec host-side cost measured in dfb9c166 (realigned: ~0 ms/exec on
identical content).

Two observations worth keeping:

- `layer31_direct.tar` and `xfce-layer31-nopanel.tar` already carry 521
  symlinks yet remain 1% aligned. They are on-disk proof of the dedup-only
  control: deduplication alone buys nothing here, alignment is the lever.

- `alpine_symlinks_preserved.tar` reads 9% but was packed BY the fixed
  packager. It has only 11 files >=64KiB, so one miss swings the percentage
  hugely; a real image packed the same way (webtop, 3055 files >=64KiB)
  measured 3055/3055 = 100%. Small-sample artefact, not a packager bug.

Recommendation: realign only what is actually used. Most entries above are
historical variants (_probe, _mimefix, _pngfix, glycin_disabled). At ~700 MB
and ~90s each, realigning all nine would burn ~6 GB for layers nobody boots.
